Privacy Policy

Version: 25 November 2026

This policy covers the website family-agenda.cloud, sign-in and the Family Agenda service. We announce material changes to families by email.

1 Controller

Artjom König, Family Agenda, Ludwigsweg 7, 53773 Hennef, Germany
Email: support@family-agenda.cloud

We are the sole controller for all processing in this policy, including the content families store in the service. We have not appointed a data protection officer; send all questions to support@family-agenda.cloud.

2 Visiting the website

When you open family-agenda.cloud, our web server processes your IP address, the time, the address requested, the browser identifier and the referrer, to deliver the page and detect attacks.

  • Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; our interest is secure operation.
  • Retention: server logs are deleted after 14 days.

The website loads nothing from third parties (fonts are on our server), sets no cookies and uses no analytics or tracking tools. If you pick the language by hand, your browser remembers that choice locally; it is not sent to us.

3 Registration and sign-in

For sign-in we process your email address, your name, a password if you set one (only as a hash), the one-time codes we send you by email, and a sign-in log with IP address and time. The sign-in service (Logto) runs on our own server.

  • Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (contract); for the sign-in log Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (preventing abuse).
  • Retention: account until the family is deleted; sign-in logs 30 days.

Preventing abuse. At registration your browser solves a small computing puzzle (CAPTCHA "Cap", on our own server, no third party). We send at most three sign-in emails per hour to one address. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

Sign in with Google. If you choose "Sign in with Google", Google sends us your name, email address and a Google identifier. Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland, is itself the controller for this; data may go to Google LLC in the US (adequacy decision EU-US Data Privacy Framework). Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR; you can use sign-in by email code instead at any time and end the link by deleting your account.

4 The service: family, sources and agendas

For the service we process what your family sets up and connects:

  • names of family members and their roles;
  • the credentials of connected sources (e.g. calendar addresses, mailbox or school portal logins), encrypted with a separate key per family;
  • the content from the sources: emails with attachments, calendar events, timetables;
  • the agenda entries made from them.

Each family has its own database on servers in Germany. We fetch from the sources on your family's behalf and do not change them.

  • Legal basis for your own data: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.
  • Legal basis for data of other people (e.g. partners, children, teachers, senders): Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; the legitimate interest is your family organising its everyday life.
  • Retention: until the family is deleted (section 8). Admins can delete single entries at any time.

AI recognition. Dates and tasks in emails and documents are recognised by an open-weight AI language model run by our host IONOS in Germany (IONOS AI Model Hub). The model does not store the content and is not trained on it. Recognition makes no decisions about people; it can be incomplete or wrong.

Special categories. Emails and calendars may incidentally contain health data (e.g. a doctor's appointment) or religious information (e.g. religious education). We process them only to show them to your family.

No own use. We show entries and content only to your family. We do not analyse them, build profiles from them or train AI models on them. We look at content only to fix a fault with an Admin's consent, or where the law requires it.

Agenda links. An agenda can be subscribed to through a secret link. Anyone who knows the link can read the agenda; Admins can revoke it at any time.

Children have no accounts of their own. Their parents enter their data.

5 People whose data a family stores

This section informs people who are not members of a family but whose data appear in a family's sources, such as teachers, clubs, email senders or calendar guests (Art. 14 GDPR). Notifying each person would be disproportionate (Art. 14(5)(b) GDPR).

  • Data: name, email address and what the message or event says.
  • Source: the family's mailbox, calendar or school portal.
  • Purpose and legal basis: display in the family's agenda, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.
  • Retention and recipients: as in sections 4, 8 and 9.
  • You can exercise your rights (section 10) via support@family-agenda.cloud.

6 Trial, subscription and payment

For the Trial and the subscription we process the contracting party's name and email address, plan, term, and the times of order, cancellation and withdrawal.

Payment is handled by Stripe Payments Europe, Limited, 1 Grand Canal Street Lower, Dublin 2, Ireland. Stripe processes payment data (card or account), billing address and country on our behalf. For fraud prevention and identity checks, Stripe is itself the controller. Data may go to Stripe, Inc. in the US (EU-US Data Privacy Framework, additionally standard contractual clauses). Stripe sets its own cookies on its payment page; Stripe's privacy policy applies to them.

  • Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR; for invoices Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR.
  • Retention: invoices and accounting records 8 years from the end of the calendar year (§ 147(3) AO, § 14b UStG); then the customer is deleted at Stripe.

7 Emails and support

We send emails that belong to the contract: sign-in codes, invitations, reminders before the Trial ends, and confirmations of order, cancellation, withdrawal and deletion. We send no newsletter and no advertising.

Emails are sent via IONOS SE, Elgendorfer Str. 57, 56410 Montabaur, Germany. We keep no copies of sent emails. The send job is deleted 30 days after delivery; our logs hold the type of email and the family, not the address. Bounce messages are deleted after reading, after 30 days at the latest. How long IONOS keeps its own logs is not known to us; IONOS's privacy policy applies.

If you write to support@family-agenda.cloud, we process your message to reply and delete it when the matter is closed, at the latest with the family.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

8 Deletion

  • Family after a Trial without order, or after the subscription ends: locked, fully deleted 30 days later
  • The family's database, key, Admin accounts: at once when the family is deleted
  • Stored files: at once; older versions after 14 days
  • Backups: 14 days after deletion at the latest
  • Server logs: 14 days
  • Sign-in logs: 30 days
  • Email send jobs, bounce messages: 30 days
  • Directory of families: an entry without name or email address remains (identifier, time zone, dates)
  • Invoices at Stripe: 8 years from the end of the calendar year

Admins can delete the family themselves at any time. Before that they can download an export.

9 Recipients

  • IONOS Cloud GmbH, Greifswalder Str. 207, 10405 Berlin, Germany: servers, storage, backups and the AI model, all in Germany. Processor.
  • IONOS SE, Elgendorfer Str. 57, 56410 Montabaur, Germany: sending email. Processor.
  • Stripe (section 6): payment processing; transfer to the US.
  • Google (section 3): only with sign-in with Google; transfer to the US.

There are no other transfers to countries outside the EU. The providers of the sources are not recipients: we fetch from them, on your family's behalf, what the family has given access to.

10 Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction (Art. 18) and data portability (Art. 20; the export in the Admin area serves this).

Objection (Art. 21 GDPR). Where we process data on the basis of Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, you can object at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation.

Write to support@family-agenda.cloud for any of these.

You can complain to a data protection supervisory authority, for example the one responsible for us: Landesbeauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit Nordrhein-Westfalen, https://www.ldi.nrw.de.

11 Further information

  • Providing your data is necessary for the contract; without it we cannot provide the service.
  • We make no automated decisions within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR.
  • Cookies: we set only cookies strictly necessary for sign-in and the session in the service (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG). That is why we do not ask for consent.
