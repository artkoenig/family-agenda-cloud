    Just connect the school timetable, your calendars and the mailbox the school writes to once. From then on Family Agenda keeps an eye on them, day and night. It only ever reads: it never posts, replies or deletes anything.

    It picks out what really matters.

    Long newsletters, scanned letters, three dates in one PDF attachment: an AI model reads them for you and pulls out what your family needs to know. What's on, what needs doing, and who it's for.

Your data

We run it, so we're responsible for it.

  • Hosted in Germany

    Your family's data lives in its own database, on servers in Germany. No ads, no tracking, and we don't sell your data.

  • Open-weight AI, run in Germany

    Your emails are read by an open-weight AI model that runs at our hosting provider in Germany. Nothing goes to an AI company, and no model is trained on what you send.

  • What we can see

    As the operator, we technically have access to your data. We look at your content only to fix an error with an Admin's consent, or when the law requires us to. If that's not for you, run Family Agenda yourself.

  • Your data stays yours

    Family Agenda never marks an email as read or touches your calendar. You can download an export of your data at any time.

Questions and answers

Frequently asked questions

Does Family Agenda work with WebUntis?

Yes. Family Agenda reads the timetable from WebUntis, including substitutions, room changes and cancellations, and shows it in each child's Agenda.

Which calendars can I connect?

Any calendar that offers an iCal (ICS) link: for example Google Calendar, iCloud or Outlook, the club calendar, school holidays or your shared family calendar.

Which mailboxes are supported?

Any mailbox with IMAP access where the school emails arrive. Family Agenda only reads: it never marks anything as read, replies or deletes.

Where is our data stored, and is it GDPR compliant?

Your data lives in its own database on servers in Germany. The AI model that reads your emails runs in Germany too. No ads, no tracking, and no model is trained on your content.

Can I try Family Agenda for free?

Yes, for 2 weeks with everything included and no payment method needed. The trial ends automatically; you only get a subscription if you sign up for one.

When can I cancel?

The monthly plan at the end of any month. The yearly plan at the end of the first year, and after that at the end of any month; we refund the unused part of the year you paid for. You can cancel online, in the Admin area or by email.

Do I get a refund if I cancel early?

Not on the monthly plan or in the first year of the yearly plan: you simply keep using Family Agenda until the end of the period you paid for. If you cancel the yearly plan after the first year, we refund the full months left of the year you paid for. And if you withdraw within 14 days of ordering, you get back everything you paid.

Our school or club uses a different app. What can I do?

Tell us which app you're missing: ideally as a request on GitHub (you'll need a free account there) or by email to support@family-agenda.cloud.

Why doesn't the cloud version read messengers like WhatsApp?

Group chats are full of messages from people outside your family. We don't want to process those on our servers without a thorough privacy review first. WhatsApp also offers no official way to read private groups, and an unofficial one can put your account at risk. If you run Family Agenda yourself, you can connect messengers at your own risk.

Can I run Family Agenda myself?

Yes. Family Agenda is open source and runs for free at home too, for example on a Raspberry Pi or a NAS.
