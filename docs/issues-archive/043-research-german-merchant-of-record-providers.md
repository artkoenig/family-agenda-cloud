**Comment by artkoenig, 2026-11-07T17:01:53Z**

Resolved in `docs/research/german-mor-providers.md` (untracked, repo has no commits yet).

Real German-law resellers: Digistore24, ablefy (reseller mode), CopeCart, affilicon, Cleverbridge. None clearly beats Paddle on least work.
- § 309 Nr. 9: Digistore24 and affilicon go open-ended with monthly cancellation after the first term; yearly billing after that unverified.
- § 312j: not solved. LG Hildesheim 11 O 5/25 held Digistore24's own button label unlawful.
- § 312k: cancel buttons at provider, own button in app still the safe plan.
- Cost: about €1.35 to €1.78 on €5 vs Paddle €0.75; €4.30 to €4.95 on €50 vs €3.00.
- Thinner webhooks, no real sandbox found, info-product checkouts, payout reserves.
Closest challenger: Digistore24. Cleverbridge is quote-based, enterprise-oriented. Comparison only, no decision.
